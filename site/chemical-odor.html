← Not sure? Find my test My home has a chemical smell

That “new” smell has a name — several, actually.

New furniture, flooring, paint and cabinetry off-gas volatile organic compounds and formaldehyde for weeks to months. A consumer monitor gives you one blended “TVOC” number; an accredited lab names the individual compounds — so a result can actually point at a source.

See recommended tests → Take the 60-sec quiz
What to test for

For an unplaceable chemical odor, the two air chemistries to measure:

  • VOCs — 25 named solvents (benzene, toluene, xylenes, limonene and more), each quantified
  • Formaldehyde & other carbonyls — the classic new-furniture off-gas
  • Ammonia / NO₂ — if the smell points to cleaning products or combustion
  • A named compound points at a source; a blended index doesn't.
