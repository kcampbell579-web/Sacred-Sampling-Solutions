Why it matters

This is the complete picture in a single test - biological, mineral, metal, volatile-organic, and forever-chemical - useful for new-home purchases, private wells you've never characterized, or anyone who wants to test everything once and keep the baseline.

Because it's collected under a single documented chain of custody, the Complete Home Inspection Water Kit also produces the most defensible record for real-estate, compliance, or dispute contexts.

How to collect your sample

Follow the sequence in the guide. Collect the most contamination-sensitive containers first - VOC vials (zero headspace) and the PFAS-free tube (ultra-clean protocol) - then the sterile bacteria bottle, then the chemistry and metals/minerals bottle. Chill everything on the gel pack and ship the same day you collect, priority.

How the lab analyzes it

Every method runs in parallel: bacteria by Colilert; nitrate, pH and hardness by IC/electrode/titration; metals by ICP-MS (200.8); chloride & fluoride by IC (300.0); VOCs by GC-MS (524.2); and PFAS by LC-MS/MS (533/537.1). The result is one report and one chain of custody covering every analyte we offer.

Did you know
  • Testing everything at once establishes a baseline you can compare against after a flood, a nearby construction project, or a change in taste or smell.
  • Each contaminant class needs its own container and technique - which is why the all-in-one kit is really several validated collections in one box.
