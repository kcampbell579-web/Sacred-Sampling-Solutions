What this tells you

Which contaminants have shown up in public, area-level records near your ZIP — from EPA and state environmental databases, water-system reports, and documented sites. It’s a smart starting point for deciding what to test first.

What it does not tell you

It does not mean any contaminant is in your home’s water or air. Area data can’t see your street, your plumbing, or your well. Only a laboratory test of a sample from your property can tell you what’s actually there.

Sources: U.S. EPA (ECHO, Superfund/NPL, SDWIS drinking-water violations, UCMR), state environmental & health agencies, and public water-system reports. Screening data compiled September 2026.
