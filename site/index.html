How Mail-In Testing Works

  1. Step 1

    Choose Your Kit

    Select a kit based on the sample you want to test and what you want the laboratory to measure.

  2. Step 2

    Activate and Collect

    Activate your kit in the customer portal, then follow the collection instructions included with your kit.

  3. Step 3

    Return Your Sample

    Follow your kit’s packaging and return-shipping instructions.

  4. Step 4

    View Your Results

    Open your digital report in the customer portal when it’s ready.

Timing: orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days, and you’ll receive tracking when your kit ships. Time in transit depends on your location. Estimated laboratory turnaround begins when the lab receives your sample — typically 7–10 business days for most kits; each kit page lists its own estimate. See our Shipping Policy.

Each kit page lists what the test measures and how the laboratory analyzes the sample. Drinking-water methods include ICP-MS for metals, GC-MS for volatile organic compounds, and LC-MS/MS for PFAS.
