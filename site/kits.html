Lab Method & Turnaround
Core (PLM)
Method
Polarized light microscopy, NYS ELAP Item 198.1
Best for
Friable materials such as popcorn ceiling, pipe and boiler insulation, plaster, drywall joint compound
Standard
Method
PLM for non-friable organically bound materials, NYS ELAP Item 198.6, with gravimetric reduction
Best for
Floor tile, mastic, roofing, caulk
Advanced (TEM)
Method
Transmission electron microscopy, NYS ELAP Item 198.4, with gravimetric reduction
Best for
Floor tile, mastic and other non-friable materials when the most definitive result is needed. In New York, a non-friable sample that is negative by PLM must be confirmed by TEM.

All asbestos kits: Analyzed by NYS ELAP-certified laboratories. Reports list asbestos type and percentage, measured against the 1% regulatory threshold.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from a hardware-store test strip?

Strips give you an approximate color read for one or two parameters. Your sample is analyzed by an accredited partner laboratory on real instruments (ICP-MS, GC-MS, LC-MS/MS) that quantify exact concentrations — for PFAS, down to parts-per-trillion — then compare each result against EPA limits. It's the difference between a guess and a measurement.

How does the mail-in process work?

Collect your sample, place the ice pack in the insulated mailer, and ship it out the same day you collect using the prepaid label. Samples are time-sensitive, so same-day shipping matters. You'll receive a digital report with a plain-language interpretation guide.

What exactly comes in the kit?

Certified sample containers (pre-cleaned and preserved), a frozen cooling gel pack, an insulated prepaid return mailer, a pre-printed sample label with a unique Sample ID, a chain-of-custody card, and step-by-step collection instructions. PFAS ships in certified PFAS-free tubes with an ultra-clean protocol.

Which kit should I choose?

Want a quick, affordable look at metals? Heavy Metals. Metals plus key minerals? Metals & Minerals. An everyday all-rounder with bacteria, nitrates, pH and hardness? Essentials (most popular). The broadest chemistry with VOCs? Comprehensive. Worried about "forever chemicals"? PFAS. Want to test everything once? Complete Home Inspection. Or take the 60-second quiz.

Can I use these results for real estate or legal purposes?

Every kit is processed under documented chain-of-custody conditions, which supports use in real estate, compliance, and dispute contexts. Kits are for informational and screening purposes and are not a medical diagnosis.

What lab method do you use for asbestos, and how long do results take?

Core kits use polarized light microscopy (PLM, NYS ELAP Item 198.1) for friable materials such as popcorn ceiling, insulation, plaster and joint compound. Standard kits use PLM for non-friable organically bound materials (NYS ELAP Item 198.6) with gravimetric reduction, for floor tile, mastic, roofing and caulk. Advanced kits use transmission electron microscopy (TEM, NYS ELAP Item 198.4) with gravimetric reduction when the most definitive result is needed; in New York, a non-friable sample that is negative by PLM must be confirmed by TEM. All asbestos samples are analyzed by NYS ELAP-certified laboratories, and reports list asbestos type and percentage against the 1% regulatory threshold. Reports are typically issued within 5–7 business days of the lab receiving your sample, plus return shipping time. Working against a deadline?
