FAQ

Questions, answered.

The things people ask before starting a project. Don't see yours? Just ask.

How long does a project take?
A marketing site is typically live in about two weeks. A full app or portal runs about four to six weeks depending on scope. You'll get a firm timeline with your fixed quote.
How does pricing work?
We scope the work up front and quote one flat price — no hourly meter, no surprise invoices. See the pricing page for typical bands, and we confirm an exact number after a short call.
Do you handle hosting?
Yes. We set up fast, secure hosting and can manage it for you on a simple monthly plan, or hand it over if you'd rather run it yourself.
What about maintenance?
Optional care plans cover hosting, updates, monitoring and a set of feature hours each month. It's month-to-month — no long contracts.
Can you redesign an existing site or app?
Often, yes. We'll audit what you have, tell you honestly whether to improve or rebuild, and give you a plan either way.
Who owns the code?
You do — completely. The code lives in your repository and the domain and accounts are in your name. No lock-in, ever.
