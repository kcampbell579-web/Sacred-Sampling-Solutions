Process

How a project actually works.

No mystery, no month-long silences. Here's exactly how we go from your first message to a live system you own.

Step 01

Discovery

We map your goals, users and must-haves, then turn them into a fixed scope and a flat quote. No guessing, no open-ended meter.

Step 02

Strategy

Sitemap, data model and technical plan. We decide what to build, what to integrate and what to leave out — before writing code.

Step 03

Design

Clickable designs you review and approve. You see and shape the product before it's built, so launch holds no surprises.

Step 04

Development

We build in visible increments on a live staging link you can watch the whole way. You're never wondering where things stand.

Step 05

Launch

Domains, analytics, testing and a final review — then we go live and hand you the keys, the code and the accounts.

Step 06

Support

Hosting, monitoring, updates and new features on a simple plan. We stay on to help the system grow with your business.

What you always get
  • A fixed scope and flat price
  • A live staging link from day one
  • Weekly, plain-English updates
  • Full ownership of code and accounts
  • A real person as your point of contact
What we ask of you
  • A short kickoff call
  • Your brand assets and content (or help creating them)
  • Timely feedback at each review
  • One decision-maker to sign off
  • Honesty about what's working
