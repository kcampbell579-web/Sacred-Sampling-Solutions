Questions

Good to know before we start.

Do I own the code and content?
Yes — completely. Everything we build is yours: the code lives in your repository, the domain and accounts are in your name, and there's no lock-in. If you ever want to bring it in-house or move on, you can.
How long does a project take?
A marketing site is typically live in about two weeks. A full app or customer portal runs about four to six weeks depending on scope. We'll give you a firm timeline with your fixed quote after a short discovery call.
Is pricing really fixed?
Yes. We scope the work up front and quote one flat price for it — no hourly meter, no surprise invoices. If you want to add something mid-project, we'll price that change clearly before doing it.
Can you take over an existing site or app?
Often, yes. We'll audit what you have, tell you honestly whether it's worth improving or rebuilding, and give you a plan either way.
What happens after launch?
You can take the keys and run it yourself, or keep us on a Command care plan for hosting, updates, monitoring, and ongoing improvements. It's month-to-month — no long contracts.
What do you need from me to start?
A short call and whatever brand assets, content, and examples you already have. If you're starting from nothing, that's fine too — helping shape it is part of the job.
